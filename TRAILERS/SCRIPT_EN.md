# Explosion Item v2 — detailed trailer script

Target: 50 seconds, 16:9, 60 FPS. Tone: safety-first escalation into controlled chaos.

## Mandatory safety preparation

- Use a disposable copy of the world. Never film in an irreplaceable build.
- Make a backup before every destructive session.
- Start far from important structures.
- Confirm the intended global `mob_griefing` value.
- Setup commands: `/function explosion_item:config/block_damage` and `/function explosion_item:config/safe_world`.
- Keep the opening safety warning visible for at least two seconds.

## Accuracy and safety rules

- Never claim safe-world mode prevents all damage; the documented promise is block protection for the creeper-based explosions.
- State that the mode changes the global `mob_griefing` gamerule.
- Do not stage explosions near real player builds.
- Do not imply that valuable items are recoverable after the fuse completes.
- Show only events produced by the release datapack.

## Reset

Stop recording, restore the disposable world backup, and check `mob_griefing` before the next take. Use `/function explosion_item:uninstall` only when preparing to remove the datapack.
